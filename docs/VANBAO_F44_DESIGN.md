# F4.4 — Ngũ Tử Phá Trận, batch 1

12 authored and verified 9×9 positions: four NOVICE (1 X move), four EASY (2 X moves), four MEDIUM (3 X moves). No HARD or MASTER puzzles are released. 88 of the 100 target positions remain unimplemented; the requested final 15/25/30/20/10 distribution is a future target, not a claim of completion. build-vanbao-puzzles-f44.cjs contains the fixed authored seed positions; no random board generator is used.

Freestyle Gomoku: at least five consecutive marks wins, even if both ends are blocked; no Renju forbidden-move rules. Every initial board has equal X/O counts and no winner. Certificates cover every legal O reply, not just the shipped opponent. Tests verify minimum forcing depth for the released tiers and reject rotation/reflection duplicates. O is deterministic: win immediately, block immediate X wins, then bounded two-ply threat/line evaluation. It never uses random weak responses.

Each approved puzzle ID has a permanent first-win record in __duduVanBaoChessF44. The submitted X-move transcript is replayed with the approved deterministic opponent. The first winning transaction fixes three offers and their shuffled order. A second transaction durably locks exactly one index; a third atomically delivers that chosen reward once. Reloads cannot reroll. Full inventory or wallet leaves the chosen reward pending; the player cannot switch boxes. Replays are free practice without another gift. No daily reset/reward system is enabled.

Reward table: NOVICE treasure ranks E/D/C, resource 6 Xu or 10 EXP; EASY E/D/C/B, resource 10 Xu or 18 EXP; MEDIUM D/C/B/A, resource 16 Xu or 30 EXP. Treasure weights retain the old pool proportions after conditioning on the permitted ranks. One chest contains treasure, one a card blank, one a resource; Fisher–Yates position shuffle. Only the chosen chest is delivered. Existing normal EXP level-up rules (including their existing Xu bonus) still apply.

All 19 old Vạn Bảo collectibles keep their IDs, metadata, images, prices and historical album records. None is converted into a card. The old 20-Xu box remains optional under Hộp mù cũ & bộ sưu tập, with its existing transaction ledger. New puzzle treasure awards retain real physical instances and confirmed album history. Three new card IDs are additive definitions: f44-card-plain E (sell 1 Xu), f44-card-spirit D (2 Xu), f44-card-star C (4 Xu). They support per-instance provenance, grouped quantity display, favorites, quantity sale and full-image viewing. Crafting/upgrade metadata is reserved; no combat, alchemy, sect or cultivation gameplay is enabled.

Same UID/profile transaction model as existing personal Spark gameplay; no Rules/Auth change or reset. Client RNG/transcript validation is honest-play client logic under the existing owner-writable Firebase architecture, not a new server-attested anti-cheat system. Unopened offers are hidden by the UI, not encrypted against an owner inspecting their own profile. Server-secret offers would require a separately approved backend change. No production Firebase writes are made by tests.

Inventory remains at 500 physical instances, favorites at 100, quantity sale at up to 100 per request; 750 KiB JSON estimate guard remains conservative. First-win/choice/claim records are not evicted. Unknown future puzzle records are preserved and incompatible metadata versions fail closed.
